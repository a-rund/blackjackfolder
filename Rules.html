Rules of the Game

Objective: Get as close to 21 as possible without exceeding it

Card Values

  1. Number cards = Face value

  2. Face cards (K, Q, J) = 10 points

  3. Aces = 1 or 11 (whichever benefits the hand)

Gameplay

  1. Players are dealt 2 cards; dealer has one face-up, one face-down.

  2. layers can Hit (draw a card), Stand (keep hand), Double Down (double bet, take one card, then stand), or Split (if starting with a pair).

  3. Try reaching as close as 21 without going over.

Winning conditions

  1. Beat dealer's hand without busting.

  2. Blackjack (Ace + 10-value card) pays 3:2.

  3. Tie = push (bet returned).

  4. Dealer bust = player wins.
